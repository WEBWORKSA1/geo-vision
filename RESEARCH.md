## Feature checklist derived

- Live layer map (quakes, natural events, ISS, terminator, multiple base maps) with side panel
- Daily auto-updating briefing (pre-rendered for SEO) + newsletter capture on every page
- Daily viral game with share grid + 4 replayable games with local bests/streaks
- Programmatic atlas (250 pages) with unique data, map, live weather, internal links
- Utility tools (distance, coordinates/UTM, sun, world clock, antipode)
- Evergreen guides targeting high-CPC topics (GIS, satellites, drone hiring, careers)
- B2B lead generation: quick form on home + 4-step qualified brief + provider supply-side form
- Monetisation stack: AdSense auto ads, sponsorships, referral fees, donations, contest sponsors
- Trust: privacy (AdSense/third-party vendor disclosures), cookies, terms, disclaimer, trademark/copyright
